8. How long your data is kept Important

worxmax is a working tool, not an archive. Data is kept as follows:

  • Projects in Cabinet Studio, Offers and Nesting, and won or lost deals in the CRM, are removed when nobody has changed them for 12 months on Office, 24 months on Workshop, and 36 months on Complete or the licence. Each change starts the time again.
  • Finished ShopFlow orders: their detailed parts lists are cleared 6 months after the order is finished; the order itself is removed after the same period as projects. Shop floor time logs are kept for the same period as projects.
  • Invoices, clients, your team and your settings are kept while your plan is active.
  • When a plan ends (section 7), everything stays readable for 60 days so you can download it, then the whole workshop is deleted for good. After a free trial that is not continued: 30 days.
  • The owner sees under Plan & billing everything due for removal in the next 30 days and can download all the workshop's data there at any time.
  • Deleted data leaves our database backups within 14 days and cannot be restored.

Keeping your own copies is your responsibility. worxmax is not a backup or archiving service. Lithuanian law requires VAT invoices to be kept for 10 years, and other laws set other periods for business records: download what you must keep before it is removed or before you stop.

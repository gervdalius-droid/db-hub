Terms of service.

The agreement between your workshop and worxmax: what you get, how you pay, how to stop, and how long your data is kept. Written to be read.

Who we are

worxmax is run by Ričardo Gervinsko įmonė, Lithuania ("we"). The service is sold to businesses: the workshop that signs up ("you"). Questions: hello@worxmax.com.

What you get

Access at app.worxmax.com to the apps in your plan, for one workshop, for as many people as you add. Office: CRM, Offers, Project Overview and Invoices. Workshop: Office plus ShopFlow, Shopfloor plan, Nesting and DXF Editor. Complete: all nine apps, including Cabinet Studio. We keep improving the apps; updates are included.

Free trial

Every new workshop gets all nine apps free for 5 days. No card is needed and nothing is charged. If you do not choose a plan, the workshop becomes read-only when the trial ends and is deleted 30 days later.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are in euro and exclude VAT. VAT is added where it applies; EU businesses with a valid VAT number are charged without it (reverse charge).
  • Monthly and yearly plans are paid in advance and renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Yearly costs ten months.
  • The Complete licence is a one-time payment for all nine apps. It includes the first year of hosting, updates and support; after that, care costs €390 a year and renews automatically. Without care the licence stays yours, but the hosted workshop becomes read-only, as below.
  • Guided setup is a one-time payment for setting up your workshop with you and importing your price lists.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store card details. Invoices are emailed and stay available under Plan & billing → Manage billing.
  • We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice by email. A new price applies from your next renewal.

Changing plan or stopping

  • Change plan, card or billing details at any time under Plan & billing → Manage billing. Moving up takes effect at once and is charged pro rata; moving down takes effect with a credit for the unused part.
  • Cancel at any time in the same place. The plan then runs until the end of the period already paid, and is not renewed. Periods already paid are not refunded.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries again and we email you. After 7 days without payment the workshop becomes read-only until it is paid.

How long your data is kept

worxmax is a working tool, not an archive. To keep it fast and to keep only what you still need, data is kept as follows:

  • Projects in Cabinet Studio, Offers and Nesting, and won or lost deals in the CRM: removed when nobody has changed them for 12 months on Office, 24 months on Workshop, and 36 months on Complete or the licence. Anything you still work on is never removed: each change starts the time again.
  • Finished ShopFlow orders: their detailed parts lists are cleared 6 months after the order is finished; the order itself is removed after the same period as projects.
  • Invoices, clients, your team and your settings: kept as long as your plan is active.
  • Shop floor time logs: kept for the same period as projects.
  • When the plan ends, after a cancellation or 7 days without payment: everything stays readable for 60 days so you can download it, then the whole workshop is deleted for good. After a free trial that is not continued: 30 days.
  • The owner sees under Plan & billing everything due for removal in the next 30 days, and can download all the workshop's data there at any time.
  • Deleted data leaves our database backups within 14 days.

You are responsible for keeping copies of records that the law requires you to keep for longer, for example invoices and accounting documents. Download them before they are removed or before you stop.

Your data is yours

Everything your workshop enters belongs to you. We use it only to provide the service to you, never sell it and never use it for advertising. For personal data in it (for example your clients' names), we act as your processor under the GDPR. The data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union (Ireland); the apps are served by Hostinger (Lithuania); payments are handled by Stripe. We may name further subprocessors with notice. See also the privacy page.

We are not liable for indirect loss such as lost profit or lost production time, except where the law does not allow this to be limited.

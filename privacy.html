What this website keeps about you.

In short: what you type into the trial form, and a few numbers about how the live demo was used. We do not sell it, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who is responsible

The company that runs worxmax is responsible for this data. To ask about it, reply to any email we sent you, or send the trial form and write your request in the note.

The trial form

When you send the form we keep:

  • your name, the name of your workshop and your email address;
  • the note you write, if any, and the plan you picked, if you came from a pricing button;
  • if you tick the box, the cabinets you built in the live demo and their example price;
  • the page you came from, your browser's name and version, and a scrambled code made from your internet address (explained below).

We use this to set up your trial, to send you the login and to contact you about the trial. The legal basis is that you asked us to: we take these steps at your request before any contract. We may email the request to ourselves so we see it quickly.

The live demo

If you change the cabinet in the demo, the page saves what you built: cabinet sizes, board colours, the number of parts and sheets, the example price, and whether you downloaded the cut list or opened the trial form. With it we keep the page you came from, the page language, your browser's name and version, and the scrambled address code.

This does not include your name or email. It is linked to your trial request only if you send the form from the same visit. We use it to see which parts of the demo people use and to improve it; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the website.

The scrambled address code

Your internet address is not stored. It is mixed with a secret value and turned into a short code that cannot be turned back into the address. We use the code only to stop one machine from sending hundreds of requests.

No cookies, no tracking

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Each visit gets a random number that lives only while the page is open. There is no advertising, no analytics service and no social media buttons.

Where the data is kept

Form requests and demo data are stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). The website itself is served by GitHub Pages, and the fonts by Google Fonts. Like any web server, those services see your internet address when your browser loads the page. We do not get that address from them.

How long

We keep a trial request for as long as we are talking with you about the trial or working together, and delete it when you ask. Demo data without a name is kept to improve the page and deleted when it is no longer useful.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we keep about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Write to us and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Lithuania that is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

# Surface: worxmax marketing site (home, apps, privacy, sign-in)

Mode: Persuade (home, apps). Privacy reads as Read; the sign-in app is Operate and takes the same world with restraint.

Audience: owners and office managers of small furniture workshops, first in Lithuania. Job: understand what worxmax does, try the live cabinet demo, ask for a free trial. Proof: the live demo and ten real app screenshots; no testimonials or customer claims exist. Constraints: static GitHub Pages, demo and intake payloads unchanged, English now with a Lithuanian version to follow. Brand commitments (user-supplied logo pack, 2026-11-25): ink #1C1A16, blue #2D5BD8 (pulled-out drawer), lime #C6F24E (drawer handle), white; Sora; the cabinet mark and wordmark are used as supplied.

Replaces the earlier Fittings Catalogue direction (yellow/graphite), which the user set aside after the new logo arrived.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is the cabinet from the worxmax logo: an ink carcass holding white drawer fronts, one per chapter, and the drawer you are in slides out blue with a lime handle. It refuses the SaaS hero-plus-feature-grid and the literal "workshop photos" page.

OWN-WORLD: warm wall grey #F2F1ED around an ink #1C1A16 carcass; white drawer fronts with rounded corners and an ink bar handle centred on top; blue #2D5BD8 only for the open or selected thing; lime #C6F24E only on handles and what you press. Sora throughout, lowercase headlines like the wordmark, tabular figures.

STORY: the visitor opens the top drawer and reads what worxmax is; the next drawer holds the live demo where one cabinet becomes a quote, cut list, sheets and labels; then the nine apps, the gains, honest prices, questions, and the trial order.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the header with the real worxmax logo and a lime trial button; below it the ink cabinet fills the width; the top drawer is pulled out to the right in blue, holding the white wordmark, the headline in lowercase Sora, one sentence and a lime "Start 5-day free trial" button; the next white drawer front (the live demo, Fig. 1) starts inside the first viewport with its handle visible.

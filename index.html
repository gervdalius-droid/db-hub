    FAQ

    Questions workshops ask first

    Which CNC machines does it work with?

    Cabinet Studio writes a drilling and routing program for each part in .XXL format, used by SCM machines, and exports the part list as .xlsx and .csv. Nesting and the DXF Editor work with DXF files, which most router and saw software can open. If your machine needs another format, tell us when you start the trial.

    Do we need to install anything?

    No. Everything runs in the browser on the office PC, a workshop tablet or the TV on the wall.

    How many people can use it?

    Everyone in your workshop, at no extra cost. Workers sign in with a name and PIN, and office apps stay hidden from shop-floor logins.

    Can we bring our own prices and materials?

    Yes. Quotes and cut lists use your own boards, edge banding, hardware and labour rates. Guided setup can import your existing price lists for you.

    Which languages?

    English and Lithuanian today, switchable per person. Need another language for your team? Tell us when you start the trial.

    Where is our data kept?

    On servers in the European Union (Ireland). Each workshop's data is kept separate from every other workshop's, and you can export it at any time.

    Can we try it first?

    Yes. Every plan starts with a 5-day free trial, so you can run a real project through it before paying.
